# Design System: gortuz.dev

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Concert Poster for Shipped Work"**

The site is a Zürich-school typographic poster. By default it is printed on warm paper; in dark mode the same poster is printed on neutral black, so the logo, the purple, and the lime are the only colour on the page. Its only image is the brand mark itself: the real logo, enlarged to poster scale, a violet square on its point cut into an indigo facet, an aubergine facet, and white slivers, outlined in near-black. Everything else is one heavy grotesk set flush-left, ragged-right, on a strict 12-column grid, separated by straight 2px rules. Work is billed like acts on a poster: the bigger the shipped system, the bigger its name.

Density is generous and rhythmic, not airy. Distances come from one 8px module (the facet unit). Two colours speak. The logo's purple is the main highlight, spent on what has to read clearly: the action, the key figures, the bullets. Lime is the second brand colour, and it means one thing: live. It marks availability, the section you are in, the entry under the pointer, keyboard focus, and a confirmed copy. Depth does not exist: no shadows, no radius, no translucent layers; on tablets and phones the big marks drop behind the text as faint watermarks, the one sanctioned use of group opacity. State is drawn with line: a broken rule is past work, a solid lime rule is live or current, a solid purple rule is the action.

The world refuses the developer-template furniture (avatar, tagline, logo-chip grid, card rows) and its terminal clichés (monospace, prompts, blinking cursors, green text on black).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light by default on warm white and beige paper; dark theme on neutral black and grey grounds, by system preference or by the header switch.
- One typeface (Archivo Variable), varied by width axis rather than by a second family.
- One module (8px), one grid (12 columns), one angle (45°).
- Purple is the main highlight; lime marks live state only, as fill or line, never as text.
- Straight lines only: rules, dashes, squares, square-capped arrows, diamond facets.
- Flat planes; zero radius; zero shadow. At 1080px and below the big marks become watermarks behind the text.
- Bilingual: Spanish at `/`, English at `/en/`, same composition.

## Colors

Two paper grounds by day and two neutral black-and-grey grounds by night, an ink family that inverts between them (aubergine on paper, neutral greys at night), the logo's purple as the main highlight, and lime as the live signal. Every colour is a CSS custom property that the dark theme reassigns; the keys ending in `-dark` in the frontmatter are the dark-theme values of the same property.

### Primary
- **Brand Purple** (`action`; `action-dark` in dark mode, the logo purple lifted for contrast on black): makes something read clearly. It carries the hero email rule (3px) and its "Escribir" label and arrow, hero fact values and work figures, work-list dashes, the contact email, and the caret.
- **Facet Violet** (`plane`, same value as light `action`, unchanged in both themes): the purple as a fill. The logo's main plane, the contact facet (both at Mark Opacity behind the text at 1080px and below), the hover fill of the language switch, theme switch, copy button, and work carousel steps, the selection background, and the scrollbar thumb on hover. Text on it is `on-fill`.

### Secondary
- **Live Lime** (`live`): the second brand colour, chosen by the user, for status and live state as a fill: the availability square in the hero and the copy button's "copied" state (with dark aubergine text, the same in both themes).
- **Live Line** (`live-ink` light, `live-ink-dark` dark): lime as a line. On paper the pure lime is too pale for a 2px line, so the line darkens to a deep leaf green; on black it is the lime itself. It draws the focus ring, the current-section nav rule (4px in the phone menu), the nav hover rule, the work-entry hover rule, the active work card's rule and current carousel tick on phones, and the outline around the status square.

### Tertiary
- **Facet Indigo** (`plane-deep`): the logo's shadow facet. Logo planes only.
- **Shade Facet** (`facet-dark` light, `facet-dark-dark` dark): the contact facet's upper plane. Aubergine on paper; indigo on the dark ground, so the facet keeps its colour against the black.

### Neutral
- **Warm Paper** (`ground`): page ground, sticky header, hero, work, contact, scrollbar track. Light theme-color.
- **Beige Paper** (`ground-alt`): the alternate band, used for the profile section and the footer.
- **Night Black** (`ground-dark`): the dark page ground, a neutral near-black with no hue. Dark theme-color (#121212, in the meta and in the manual-switch sync).
- **Night Grey** (`ground-alt-dark`): the dark alternate band (profile, footer), a neutral grey one step lighter.
- **Aubergine Ink** (`ink`, also `rule`) / **Night White** (`ink-dark`, also `rule-dark`, neutral): headings, the role line, primary links, 2px structural rules, the phone menu's link rules, the menu lines, the contact email rule (3px), button borders, the skip-link block.
- **Soft Ink** (`ink-soft` / `ink-soft-dark`): running body text, the status line, fact captions, footer line.
- **Muted Ink** (`ink-muted` / `ink-muted-dark`): places, dates, figure labels, the work stack line, the diamond outline that stands in for a missing technology mark, the scrollbar thumb.
- **Quiet Rule** (`rule-quiet` / `rule-quiet-dark`): 1px subdividers inside a block (header base, footer top, record rows, the stack line), and the 2px idle carousel ticks.
- **Facet Edge** (`edge`): the logo's outline; facet edges inside the logo and the contact facet. Never text.
- **On Fill** (`on-fill`): text on a purple fill (switch and button hover, selection).
- **Sliver White** (`white`): the logo's white slivers.

### Mark Opacity
- **Mark Opacity** (`--mark-opacity`: 0.16 light, 0.3 dark): the group opacity of the big logo plane and the contact facet when they sit behind the text at 1080px and below. Not a colour; it lives in the sidecar's extensions and the dark theme reassigns it like any other property. The dark value is higher because the facets sink into the black ground.

### Named Rules
**The Paper Ground Rule.** In the light theme, grounds are warm white or beige, nothing else. Purple and lime appear as small fills, lines, and type, or as a facet plane; neither ever becomes a section background.

**The Neutral Night Rule.** The dark theme's grounds and inks are neutral black and greys with no hue; the logo, the purple, and the lime are the only colour on a dark page. Never tint a dark ground or ink toward violet or aubergine, and never use the violet or the indigo as a ground. Every dark value is a reassignment of an existing custom property; components never branch on theme.

**The Clear Purple Rule.** Purple is the main highlight: what you can act on and the figures that carry the argument. If an element is neither, it wears ink.

**The Live Rule.** Lime means live, and only live: available now, the section on screen, the thing under the pointer or the keyboard, the copy that just succeeded. It is a fill (`live`) or a line (`live-ink`), never running text, never a section ground.

**The Logo-Plus-Lime Rule.** Every hue comes from the logo's facets or is a tint of the same aubergine (the light theme's inks), plus the one lime. The grounds and the dark theme's neutral greys are the only other colours. Technology marks never bring their brand colours; they take the text colour (`currentColor`) in both themes.

### Named Rules
**The One Face Rule.** Archivo Variable is the only family. Contrast is made with the width axis (75 / 100 / 125) and weight, never with a second typeface or a monospace.

**The Billing Rule.** A work entry's title size is set by how much of the system was shipped (weight 3, 2, 1), not by recency.

## Layout

A 12-column grid (`repeat(12, minmax(0, 1fr))`) inside a 1440px max container, with outer margins of clamp(16px, 6.6vw, 96px) and a 32px gutter (16px at 760px and below). Composition is flush-left: headlines start on column 1, text blocks span 5 to 9 columns, and meta columns sit in columns 1 to 3 against a body on columns 4 to 12.

Spacing is built from the 8px module, in whole and half steps: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 80, 96, 128. Sections breathe at 96px to 128px vertically on desktop, 64px to 80px on phones. Sections alternate grounds: ground (hero, work), alternate (profile, with a 2px rule on top), ground (contact, with a 2px rule on top), alternate (footer, with a 1px quiet rule on top). The alternation holds in both themes.

Hero: at 1081px and up the whole logo sits absolutely on the right at poster scale (width min(62vw, 1000px), right offset max(-18vw, -300px), vertically centred), while name, role, status, facts, and action hold columns 1 to 6, so text and logo never overlap. Rhythm under the name: role at 32px, status at 20px, facts at 48px, action at 40px (24 / 16 / 40 / 32 on short laptops). At 1080px and below the logo moves behind the text as a watermark: absolutely positioned top-right (width min(72vw, 640px), right -16px, top 16px) at Mark Opacity, z-index 0, with the text content above it at z-index 1; the hero drops its full-height centring and opens with 64px of top padding. Name, facts, and action take 10 columns, role and status 8. At 760px and below the watermark grows to 104vw wide, right -18vw, top 8px, so the right edge crops it; everything is full width, the facts stack to one column, and the email action follows the status before the facts.

Work: at 760px and below the stacked entries become a horizontal swipe carousel. The track sits in the content column; each card is 100% of the content width, with a gap of twice the page margin so no neighbour peeks in, snapping is mandatory on the x axis, and every card stops the swipe (`scroll-snap-stop: always`). Carousel controls sit above the cards with 16px below them. Desktop and tablet keep the stacked list.

Profile: stack items are rows (16px icon, 10px gap, name) with 5px vertical padding. At 760px and below the stack groups become one group per row (24px apart), and each group's items run inline, wrapping, with a 20px column gap and no separators; the icons separate them.

Header: at 760px and below the inline links and controls give way to the menu button; the bar stays 72px.

Contact: at 1080px and below the facet keeps its place on the right edge but drops behind the text at Mark Opacity, with the content above it. On phones the section pads 80px top and bottom and the facet is 76% of the section height, starting 12% down.

Breakpoints: 1180px (stack groups 5 to 3 columns), 1080px (tablet), 760px (phone: menu panel, work carousel, inline stack groups). A short-laptop query (761px and up, 820px tall and below) tightens hero rhythm so the email stays in the first screen. The grid rescales; it never breaks into a different system.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat in both themes. There are no shadows, no blurs, no translucent overlays. Depth is replaced by planes laid side by side (the logo's facets, the ground bands) and by line weight: 4px for the current item on phones (menu section rule, carousel tick), 3px for the action and contact-email rules, 2px for structure and state, 1px quiet rules for subdivision. The phone menu panel is not a layer over the page but an opaque ground-colour sheet under the bar. Opacity has two uses, neither of them a translucent surface: the phone carousel, where inactive cards recede to 40% as a focus cue (no scale), and the watermarks, where the big logo plane and the contact facet sit behind the text at Mark Opacity on screens 1080px and below.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Plane Rule.** Nothing floats. Surfaces differ only by flat fill and are separated only by rules. One deliberate exception, chosen by the user: at 1080px and below the big marks (hero logo, contact facet) sit behind the text at Mark Opacity, as group opacity on the whole mark, never as a translucent panel or a tint on a surface. At 1081px and up they stay full-colour planes beside the text.

## Shapes

Square corners everywhere (0px radius), including the copy button and both header switches. Lines are straight: 2px rules over facts, records, stack groups, and section tops; a dashed 2px rule (24px dash, 8px gap) over each work entry; 16px by 2px purple dashes as list bullets; arrows drawn with 2px square-capped strokes and miter joins. The live marker is a square, not a dot: 8px of lime with a 1px live-line outline offset 2px.

The only diagonal is 45° in the world's own drawing (published technology marks are reproduced as their makers drew them, see Stack item): the logo is a square on its point, the contact facet is a half-diamond in a fixed 1:2 box so its edges stay at exactly 45°, the theme switch's icon is a square cut on its 45° diagonal, the outbound-link arrow is rotated 45°, the menu button's outer lines cross into an X on the 45° axis, and the facet entrance travels along the 45° axis.

### Named Rules
**The State-by-Line Rule.** A broken rule means past or archived; a solid lime rule means live or current; a solid purple rule means the action. Links and entries draw their lime rule in from the left on hover; the current nav section keeps it. Only the square controls (language switch, theme switch, copy button, carousel steps) fill: purple on hover, lime when copying succeeds.

**The One Angle Rule.** 45° is the only diagonal. Everything else is horizontal or vertical.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** square, 2px ink border (0px radius).
- **Outline (copy email):** transparent fill, ink text, 15px weight 700, 8px by 16px padding.
- **Hover:** fills Facet Violet with a violet border and on-fill text over 200ms ease-out.
- **Copied:** fills Live Lime with a lime border and dark aubergine text; the confirmation text replaces the label for 2.4s and is announced through a polite live region. A failed copy shows the failure text without the lime.
- **Focus:** global 2px live-line outline at 4px offset.

### Action line
The primary call to action is not a button: the email address in ink at the Action size, over a 3px purple rule spanning six columns, with the "Escribir" label and a straight arrow in purple at the far end. On hover the arrow moves 6px right and the address underlines at 2px. On phones label and arrow drop under the address. ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** derive every distance from the 8px module and place every block on the 12-column grid.
- **Do** set every light section on warm white (#fbf9f5) or beige (#f2ede4) paper, and every dark section on neutral black (#121212) or grey (#1c1c1c).
- **Do** add new colours as custom properties with a value for each theme; components read the property and never branch on theme.
- **Do** separate content with straight 2px rules; use 1px quiet rules only to subdivide inside a block.
- **Do** draw state with line: dashed for past work, solid lime for live or current, purple for the action.
- **Do** spend the brand purple on what must read clearly: actions, key figures, bullets, the caret.
- **Do** spend lime only on live state: availability, the current section, hover and focus, a confirmed copy.
- **Do** set new hierarchy with Archivo's width axis (125 for section heads, 75 for figures) before adding size.
- **Do** build any new image from the logo's construction as flat facet planes on 45°.
- **Do** set a big mark behind text only at 1080px and below, as group opacity at `--mark-opacity` with the text above it; at desktop widths keep it full colour beside the text.
- **Do** give every motion a reduced-motion state that shows the end frame at rest.
- **Do** give every phone touch control a 44px square hit area.
- **Do** ship every surface in Spanish at `/` and English at `/en/` with the same composition.

### Don't:
- **Don't** fill a section ground with the violet, the indigo, or lime; in dark mode the ground is neutral black or grey only, never tinted toward violet or aubergine.
- **Don't** set running body text in purple; body is soft ink.
- **Don't** set any text in lime; lime is a fill or a line, and text on a lime fill is dark aubergine.
- **Don't** use lime for emphasis that isn't live state; emphasis is purple.
- **Don't** round corners, add shadows, or use translucent glass layers; the Mark Opacity watermark is the only opacity on a mark, and it never applies to a surface.
- **Don't** wrap content in cards or set labels and tags as pills.
- **Don't** use colour gradients; the only linear-gradient allowed is the hard-stop dash pattern that draws a broken rule.
- **Don't** add a second typeface, a monospace, or terminal devices (prompts, blinking cursors, green text on black).
- **Don't** introduce any hue outside the logo's facets, their aubergine tints, the one lime, and the grounds.
- **Don't** put small uppercase labels (eyebrows) above headings; a heading stands on its own rule.
- **Don't** mark live state with a dot or a circle; the live marker is a square.
- **Don't** use any diagonal other than 45°, or curved lines, in the world's own drawing; published technology marks keep their own shapes.
- **Don't** give a technology mark its brand colour or a chip; it is a 16px `currentColor` mark beside its name.
